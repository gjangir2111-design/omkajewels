Are your products nickel-free and hypoallergenic? +
Yes, our entire collection is crafted from high-quality, nickel-free alloys and is suitable for sensitive skin. We use only skin-safe materials.
How do I take care of my artificial jewellery? +
Keep your jewellery away from water, perfume and chemicals. Store in a cool, dry place, preferably in the pouch or box provided. Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth after use.
Can I change or cancel my order? +
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 12 hours of placing them. After that, the order may be dispatched and cannot be changed. Contact us immediately at 7357869607.
Will colours look exactly like pictures? +
We make every effort to photograph products accurately. Slight colour variations may occur due to different screen settings. Product descriptions mention exact materials and plating.
Do you offer gift packaging? +
Yes! We offer premium gift packaging on request. Please mention it in the order notes at checkout. Gift packaging is free for orders above ₹999.
How long does shipping take? +
Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days. Express delivery (2–3 business days) is available for select pin codes at an additional charge of ₹79.

Shipping Policy

We ship all across India through trusted courier partners including Delhivery, DTDC, Blue Dart, Ekart, and India Post.

  • Free Shipping on all orders above ₹499.
  • ₹49 Delivery Charge on orders below ₹499.
  • Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Standard delivery: 5–7 business days across India.
  • Express delivery: 2–3 business days (available at extra charge of ₹79).
  • Delivery to remote areas may take 7–10 business days.
  • Once dispatched, a tracking ID will be shared via SMS and email.
  • We currently do not ship internationally.

Order Tracking

You can track your order anytime using the "Track Order" section on our website. Enter your AWB / Tracking number to get real-time updates from the courier company.

Return Policy

We want you to love every purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, we offer a hassle-free return within 7 days of delivery.

  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in original packaging with all tags intact.
  • Damaged or defective products will be replaced or fully refunded.
  • Sale / discounted items are not eligible for return unless defective.
  • Customised or personalised items cannot be returned.
  • Return pickup is free for defective products. For other returns, return shipping cost is borne by the customer.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the return is received and inspected.

Exchange Policy

  • Exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
  • Size exchanges are subject to stock availability.
  • Colour / design exchanges will be treated as a return and re-order.
  • Contact our customer care at 7357869607 to initiate an exchange.
  • Exchange shipping charges may apply depending on the reason for exchange.

How to Initiate a Return

  • Call or WhatsApp us at 7357869607 within 7 days of delivery.
  • Provide your order ID and reason for return.
  • Our team will arrange a pickup within 2–3 business days.
  • Refund / exchange will be processed after quality check.
